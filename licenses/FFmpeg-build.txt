  scripts/build-bundled-ffmpeg.sh /absolute/path/to/build-directory
The script verifies the archive SHA-256 before building.
The binaries statically link FFmpeg libraries and dynamically link only Apple
system libraries; they do not require Homebrew or a separately installed FFmpeg.
Stream copy is supported; media downloading is handled by yt-dlp.
